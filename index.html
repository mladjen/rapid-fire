The Password-style party game

How to play

1
You are the Clue-Giver — this laptop is your private screen. Put the audience board on the big screen for the guessers.
2
10 words with first letters running in alphabetical order. The board shows the guessers the letter & blanks — never the answer.
3
Describe each word without saying it — no part of the word, spelling, or rhymes.
4
Hit ✓ Got it, or Pass to skip — skipped words come back around while the clock runs, so you can return to them.
